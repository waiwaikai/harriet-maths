# Harriet's Daily Maths — Build Requirements

**Version:** 1.0 (build brief for Claude Code)
**Owner:** YY (parent)
**Purpose:** A daily 10-minute morning maths routine for a Year 2 child, running from July to December 2026, that consolidates NSW Stage 1 content and moves confidently into Stage 2 — while managing frustration and building confidence.

---

## 1. Context & success definition

**Who it's for.** Harriet, Year 2 (NSW), finishing Stage 1 and moving into Stage 2. She is **top of her class in literacy but middle-of-the-pack in numeracy**. Her teacher is surprised by the gap. Working hypothesis: the bottleneck is **frustration tolerance, not ability** — she is used to things coming easily in her strong subject and disengages when a maths concept doesn't click immediately. Design accordingly.

**Honest success definition.** By Christmas: Harriet is **confident and working into Stage 2 content**, and can **recall the full run of the year's material** (not just recent weeks). This is explicitly **not** full Year 3 / Stage 3 mastery — that is unrealistic on 10 min/day, and chasing it would feed the frustration this app exists to manage. Success = she *enjoys* the morning, her **first-time-right rate climbs over the term**, and the numeracy gap narrows.

**Design north stars.**
1. Protect confidence above all — a wrong answer must never land as failure.
2. First-time-right is the only true mastery signal; everything else is a soft pass.
3. Use her literacy strength as the on-ramp into numeracy (story-framed problems).
4. Keep the parent in the loop in the default mode; solo mode is an exception, not an equal substitute.

---

## 2. Pedagogical foundations (evidence-based — build these in, don't decorate with them)

- **Gradual release ("I do / we do / you do")** — the session's core structure. This is the format that already works for Harriet.
- **Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract (CPA)** — demonstrations lead with a visual/concrete model before symbols.
- **Retrieval + spaced + interleaved practice ("desirable difficulties")** — the mechanism behind the daily warm-up, Friday revision, and expanding recall pool.
- **Conceptual + procedural taught together** — fluency is understanding *plus* automaticity, never rote.
- **Cognitive load management** — one new concept per session; worked examples; clean, uncluttered presentation; audio support to remove reading load in solo mode.

---

**Holiday:** Sat 26 Sep → Mon 12 Oct (routine optionally pauses; the spaced-recall logic handles re-entry — a gap is not lost time).
**Term 4:** Tue 13 Oct → Thu 17 Dec (Week 1 is a 4-day week; Week 10 is a 4-day Mon–Thu week). 10 weeks.

**Diagnostic:** run a one-session placement diagnostic **before Term 3 starts** to set Harriet's true starting point on the spine (see §8). Do not assume her entry level.

The through-line is **Number & Algebra** (where she's middling and what compounds). Measurement / Space / Data are woven in lighter, not blocked separately. ## 4. Daily rhythm

- **Mon–Thu:** warm-up recall → demonstration → we-do → her independent 10.
- **Friday:** revision/consolidation — **no new concept**; wider recall sweep.
- Total ~10 min; treat as a guide, not a rigid timer. Rough split: warm-up ~2 min, demo ~2 min, we-do ~3 min, independent 10 ~3 min.

### Session structure (both modes)
1. **Warm-up recall** — 2–3 questions from the spaced pool (§5). Always includes at least one guaranteed win to open on success.
2. **"I do" (demonstration)** — one worked example of today's concept, **visual/CPA-led**.
3. **"We do"** — **two worked examples** for the parent to run through *with* Harriet. (See §6 — these are generated for the parent, who may need the support too.)
4. **"You do"** — Harriet's **10 independent questions** on today's concept.

---

## 5. Spaced-recall engine (the "recall all by December" mechanism)

The daily warm-up draws 2–3 questions from **three rotating buckets**:
- **Last week**
- **~3–4 weeks ago**
- **Term-start** (this bucket **grows** across the year, so by December the pool spans everything covered).

Friday sweeps wider across all buckets. Concepts flagged as soft (second-looks) or gaps (parks) get **up-weighted** in the coming days' warm-ups and Friday revision — quiet re-emphasis, never singling her out.

---

## 6. Two modes

### Together mode (default)
- App is **co-pilot**: serves the demo, generates the two we-do examples, generates the independent 10, tracks mastery.
- **Parent runs the "we do"** — the responsive co-regulation that does the real confidence work.
- **We-do parent examples:** generate **2 worked examples with step-by-step guidance for the parent**, so YY can run them confidently even on a concept she's rusty on. Each should include: the problem, the concrete/visual model to use, the steps to talk through, and the answer.
- **Scoring:** the independent 10 are **voice-scored** (same as solo) for consistency — Harriet reads her answers aloud at the end.

### Solo mode (exception — off-mornings; Harriet opens it herself on her iPad)
- App runs all three parts.
- **Demonstration:** visual.
- **Independent 10:** **audio-only** (questions read aloud; removes reading load). Harriet works on her **whiteboard**.
- **Answering:** at the end, she **reads all 10 answers aloud**; app scores by voice. (No handwriting recognition — voice is the reliable channel.)
- **Photo of workings:** optional, audit-trail-for-parent only — **never** part of marking. Do not build a growing image archive; if captured, it's for same-session parent review, not accumulation.

---

## 7. Marking & the "look again" loop (both modes)

Never say "wrong." Per incorrect answer:
1. **First miss →** warm prompt: *"Have another look — check your workings carefully."* Send her back to **rework** it on the whiteboard (not just re-read), then re-say the answer.
2. **One retry only.** A second nudge on the same question becomes the "wrong" signal we're avoiding.
3. **Still off →** park it warmly: *"Let's look at this one together with umma later."* Move on. No looping.

**Scoring tiers (feed the adaptivity engine — §8):**
- **First-time-right** → true mastery signal.
- **Right after a look** → counts as **right for Harriet's confidence** (she gets the win), but **logged separately as "needed a second look"** — a soft pass, not mastery.
- **Parked** → a genuine gap.

---

## 8. Adaptivity rules

Scored per concept from the independent 10, using the three tiers above.

**Diagnostic (pre-term):** a single placement session to locate Harriet on the spine before Term 3. Use it to set starting week and flag any Stage 1 gaps to front-load.

**Triggers (defaults — tune after diagnostic):**
- **Advance** a concept: **≥8/10 first-time-right across two sessions.**
- **Hold & consolidate** (repeat with fresh questions, don't advance): **5–7/10**, or a high proportion of second-looks.
- **Reteach** (back to demo with a **new representation**): **≤4/10**, or **parked twice**.
- **Too easy / skip-ahead:** **≥9/10 first-time-right immediately** → compress (skip consolidation days) or branch to a **depth/extension** version — not more of the same.
- **Missed days (incl. holiday):** don't drop content — **shift the sequence forward**; on return, auto-insert a recall warm-up of the last concept before resuming.

Second-looks and parks feed forward into warm-up and revision weighting (§5).

---

## 9. Frustration-tolerance layer (first-class feature)

- **Open on a guaranteed win** every session.
- **One stretch concept per session** — respect the cognitive-load cap.
- **Name the struggle** — normalise being stuck ("getting stuck is where your brain grows"). Never frame errors as failure.
- **Visible progress** — streak and progress indicators she can see.
- **Literacy on-ramp** — frame number work as stories ("tell me the story of this sum") to pull her in through her strong door.

---

## 10. Data & persistence

Persist (text/JSON; **not** images):
- Per-concept status + history.
- Daily session log: date, mode, score, **first-time-right count**, second-looks, parks.
- Streak; current position in the spine.
- Diagnostic result.

Parent-facing view: per-concept mastery, trend in first-time-right rate, list of parked concepts to revisit, and (same-session only) any uploaded workings photos.

## 11. Technical notes & risks (for Claude Code)

- **Target device:** iPad (Harriet opens solo mode herself). Design for touch + audio; large tap targets; minimal text on-screen in solo mode.
- **Audio out (questions read aloud):** browser **SpeechSynthesis** (TTS) is well-supported on iPad Safari — safe to rely on.
- **Voice in (reading answers aloud) — BIGGEST UNKNOWN:** speech-to-text (`webkitSpeechRecognition` / Web Speech API) support on iOS Safari is **inconsistent and should be tested early**. Build a **fallback**: if recognition is unreliable, let her tap answers on a large number pad, or have the app read each answer back for a tap-to-confirm. Do not ship voice-only without a fallback.
- **Question generation:** generating fresh, adaptive, curriculum-aligned questions each day suits an LLM call. If built with API-generated questions, **cache a fallback bank** so a bad connection on a school morning never blocks the routine. Alternatively pre-generate per-week banks.
- **Number recognition of handwriting:** explicitly **out of scope** — voice replaced it. Photos are audit-trail only.
- **Reliability:** the morning routine must start fast and never hang. Prefer local-first with graceful degradation over anything that blocks on the network.

---

## 12. Terminology & tone

- Use **"umma"** (Korean for mother) wherever the app refers to the parent — e.g., *"Let's look at this one with umma later."*
- Warm, encouraging, age-6 tone. Short sentences. Never clinical, never "wrong/incorrect/fail."

---

## 13. Out of scope (v1)

- Full Stage 3 / Year 3 mastery.
- Handwriting/photo-based marking.
- Persistent image storage.
- Multi-child support.
- Any social/leaderboard features.
